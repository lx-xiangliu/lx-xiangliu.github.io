About Me

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Xiamen University and a research intern at Stony Brook University.

My research focuses on AI for medicine and healthcare, particularly medical foundation models, clinical reasoning, agentic retrieval, and multimodal biomedical representation learning. My recent work spans disease-centric clinical case retrieval, medical terminology interoperability, and foundation models for neuroimaging data.

In the long term, I am interested in fundamental machine learning methods that enable reliable representation learning, reasoning, and generalization across biomedical tasks and modalities.

Selected Research

01NeurIPS 2026

Agentic Retrieval for Clinical Case Search

A trajectory-memory-based framework for improving agentic retrieval in disease-centric clinical case search.

My contribution. Research, method development, and experimental evaluation for disease-centric clinical case retrieval and trajectory memory.

02Under review

Online Evidence-Containment Prediction over Text Spans under Distinct Queries

A framework for predicting whether nested text spans contain complete evidence before verification. Online Evidence-Containment Prediction (OECP) connects span containment to online isotonic regression and uses Expected Search-space Reduction (ESR) to guide efficient evidence localization.

Research focus. Online learning, structured prediction, and budgeted evidence verification for question answering and fact verification.

032026 – Present · Ongoing

Foundation Models for Functional Neuroimaging

Exploring scalable foundation model architectures for learning representations from functional MRI time-series data.

Current focus. fMRI data structures and preprocessing, large-scale neuroimaging representation learning, Transformer-based temporal models, cross-subject generalization, and downstream evaluation.
